Beyond one delta

Mangroves connect to five of the UN's global goals.

The Sustainable Development Goals set 17 targets for 2030. Watching this one coast touches these five.

SDG 6 · Clean Water

Residents describe polluted water in the creeks. This project tracks the mangroves that depend on that water. It does not measure pollution itself.

sdgs.un.org ↗

SDG 13 · Climate Action

Mangrove soil and roots store carbon. If the trees are lost, that stored carbon can be released.

sdgs.un.org ↗

SDG 14 · Life Below Water

Mangroves are habitat for many coastal and marine species. Tracking their change helps show where that habitat is shrinking.

sdgs.un.org ↗

SDG 15 · Life on Land

Mangroves are coastal forests. Radar can still record them when clouds block optical satellites, so the view is less often lost.

sdgs.un.org ↗

SDG 17 · Partnerships

We work with local organizations, including Saltwater Marina and Mangrove Crew, so the satellite data stays tied to what people see on the coast.

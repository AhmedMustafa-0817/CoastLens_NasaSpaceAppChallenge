Finding 03 · NISAR

A year of radar: a steady echo, a restless surface.

NISAR passed every 12 days. Top: how bright each kind of surface looked. If the forest were dying, the mangrove line would sink toward the water line. It did not.

Bottom: coherence, or how alike two passes 12 days apart were. 1 means nothing moved. The mangrove surface was very stable in winter, unsettled in May and June, then steady again.

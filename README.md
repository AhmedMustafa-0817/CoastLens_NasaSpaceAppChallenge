# CoastLens

**Watching Karachi's mangroves from space, so conservation teams know where to look first.**

🏆 **Local Runner-Up, NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026** · Challenge: *Dancing with the SARs*

🌐 **Live site:** https://coast-lens-nasa-space-app-challenge.vercel.app

## What it is

CoastLens is a web tool that monitors the mangrove forests of Karachi's coast, from Rehri Goth to Port Qasim, using NASA-ISRO's NISAR radar satellite.

A mangrove forest can look thick and green from above while it is thinning inside. Optical satellites only see the canopy, and the monsoon can hide this coast for weeks. NISAR's L-band radar works through cloud and at night, and its long wavelength passes the leaves and reflects off trunks and branches. If the forest weakens inside, the echo weakens too. NISAR returns every twelve days.

## What we found

- Mangroves in our study area have grown overall since 1985, but in **seven zones** the forest kept shrinking.
- In one year of NISAR data, we flagged **eight patches** where the radar echo faded.
- Together they cover about **63 hectares out of roughly 18,000**. That is where a field team goes first.

Click any flagged patch on the map to see the evidence behind it.

## Limits

Radar shows where the echo changed, not why. Tide, rain and wet mud also move the signal. Every alert is a question for a field visit, not a verdict, and none has been verified on the ground yet. One year of data is not yet a trend.
